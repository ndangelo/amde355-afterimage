The exhibition

A visual study of light, time, and the landscapes we carry with us.

Photography as an act of noticing.

Afterimage is a fictional exhibition created as a teaching example. It considers the way familiar environments change with light and atmosphere.

Its photographs invite viewers to slow down and make their own associations.

Curatorial themes

  • Color and emotion
  • Document and abstraction
  • Changes in everyday places
